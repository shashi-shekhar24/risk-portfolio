# Claims audit (public-safe)

This repository is public, so this file lists only what the site publishes. Each claim follows the four
rules in `nda-safe-case-study-guide.md`: methodology over metrics, problem class not portfolio, relative
not absolute figures, and Context / Diagnosis / Intervention / Outcome.

| Published claim | Why it is safe |
|---|---|
| $2B+ business lending book | Rounded order of magnitude; Shashi chose $2B+ over the guide's >$1B on 8 Oct 2026 |
| Open Banking underwriting launched in the UK and US; UK rules adopted unchanged | No dates, volumes, thresholds or rule content |
| Data-extraction failures eliminated within weeks of launch | No rate, count or vendor |
| Eligibility up 3%, $150MM more booked, vintage loss rate 2% lower than the prior rule strategy | Changes only, no baselines or levels. The $150MM is a dollar amount the guide would exclude; Shashi chose to publish it on 9 Oct 2026 |
| Credit policy method: buy box, swap-set analysis, balance-controlled GCL, risk grades by t-statistics | Method only; no buy-box criteria, grade boundaries or loss levels |
| Vintage loss rate down 2.5%, realised profit uplift | Change only, no baseline, level or profit amount; unit deliberately left unstated |
| Multi-horizon default definition, champion-challenger, stability monitoring | Method only; horizons and thresholds not given |
| CLTV framework: performance curves by chain-ladder, margin stack | Method only; no curve count, horizon or margin inputs |
| Decision engine: internal models plus bureau scores, three paths | Structure only; no model names, counts or cutoffs |
| Liquiloans 10%, 0.5%, 30%; Jodo 40%, 80%; Accelera 10% | Relative figures only |
| Team sizes and dates of employment | Personal career facts |

## Never publish
Dollar amounts (other than the $2B+ and $150MM approved above), launch dates, adoption or error rates, loss-rate levels, model counts and names,
internal project names, vendor names, committee names, cutoffs, and anything from internal reviews.

## Before adding anything
Run the four-question checklist at the end of `nda-safe-case-study-guide.md`. This file and the guide
are a working standard, not legal advice. The confidentiality agreements themselves decide what is allowed.
