# Claims audit (public-safe)

This repository is public, so this file lists only what the site publishes. Each claim follows the rules in
`nda-safe-case-study-guide.md`: methodology over metrics, problem class not portfolio, changes not levels,
and Context / Diagnosis / Intervention / Outcome.

| Published claim | Why it is safe |
|---|---|
| Multi-billion-dollar US and UK small-business lending book | Scale only, no figure |
| Every strategy cleared second-line risk, model risk management, legal and compliance review | States that reviews happened; nothing from their content |
| Credit policy redesign: post-bureau eligibility +3 pp, more loans booked, portfolio vintage loss rate −200 bps | Changes only; no baselines, levels or volumes |
| Credit policy method: swap-set analysis, balance-controlled GCL, risk grades | Method only; no grading statistics, grade boundaries or loss levels |
| Multi-horizon default definition: bank-based underwriting vintage loss rate −250 bps | Change only; no baseline or level |
| Multi-horizon diagnosis: early delinquents that cured, and late deterioration | Mechanism only; horizons and thresholds not given |
| Open Banking underwriting launched in the UK and US; US failures eliminated within weeks | No dates, volumes, thresholds or rule content |
| US Open Banking straight-through rate of 82% | An operating level, not a risk or loss level; published at the owner's choice |
| CLTV: chain-ladder curves, NPV and ROI per account, cut-offs and pricing | Method only; no horizon, curve count, margin inputs or returns |
| Decision engine: internal PD models plus bureau scores, three paths | Structure only; no model names, counts or cutoffs |
| Liquiloans approval +10 pp, NPA −50 bps, collection efficiency +30%; Jodo 40%, 80%; Accelera 10% | Changes only, no baselines |
| Team sizes and dates of employment | Personal career facts |

## Never publish
Dollar amounts of any kind (book size, volumes, profit), launch dates, loss-rate or default-rate levels,
error or adoption rates other than the straight-through rate above, model counts and names, internal project
names, vendor names, committee names, cutoffs, and the content of any internal review.

## Before adding anything
Run the four-question checklist at the end of `nda-safe-case-study-guide.md`. This file and the guide
are a working standard, not legal advice. The confidentiality agreements themselves decide what is allowed.
