Risk Philosophy

Views on Credit Architecture.

Positions held through production experience—not textbook theory.

◆

Aggregation is a lossy transformation

A simple sum of bank statement data destroys the temporal signal. Credit risk is often a liquidity timing problem; Signal processing (FFT) preserves what rolling averages mask.

◇

Bureau data is a floor, not a ceiling

Traditional scores tell you who defaulted in the past. Transaction data tells you who is approaching a cashflow crunch now. The former identifies bad risk; the latter manages it.

▲

Model risk is often an execution gap

A "perfect" PD model with a flawed interest-accrual or split architecture at the final EMI will consistently fail on reconciliation. Infrastructure is part of the risk model.

○

Explainability (SHAP) is for Governance

Feature importance doesn't guarantee fairness, but it ensures auditability. In a $3B+ portfolio, the ability to defend a single decline to a regulator is as critical as the model's Gini.
